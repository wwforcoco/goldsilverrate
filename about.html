About GoldSilverRate.pk

Sarafa Bazar benchmark · Updated:

What this site is

GoldSilverRate.pk is an independent information website for the Pakistani Sarafa market. We publish the daily benchmark rates for gold and silver in both lines that actually matter to buyers: the selling rate a counter charges and the buying rate a dealer pays. Every number on this site flows from one central data file, so the homepage, the city pages, the bar pages and the calculator never disagree with each other.

We cover the weights the bazaar actually trades in: tola, masha and ratti alongside grams; single tola bars up to 10 tola sealed bars, 52 tola bulk lots and kilogram bars; and the difference between certified 999 fine metal and local Rawa.

What we are not

We are not a jeweller, a bullion dealer or an investment adviser. We do not buy, sell, store or broker any metal, and we do not handle money. The rates we publish are market benchmarks for general information, and the final rate of any deal is what you and your counter agree. Our full terms are on the disclaimer page.

Our working rules

  • One data file, updated daily, powering every page and calculation.
  • Both market lines shown: selling and buying, never one without the other.
  • Plain language, short sentences, tables a phone screen can actually read.
  • Wrong number reported through the contact page gets fixed the same day.
