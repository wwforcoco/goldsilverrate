About GoldSilverRate.pk

GoldSilverRate.pk is a simple website that shows daily gold and silver rates for Pakistan. We built it because most people cannot find the exact price for the weight they want to buy, such as 10 tola, 52 tola or 1 kilo, in one clear place.

What we do

We take the day's benchmark rate from the Sarafa Bazar and use one fixed formula to work out every weight: 1 tola = 11.664 grams. All pages use the same numbers, so rates never differ from page to page.

How often we update

Rates are updated on working days. The time of the last update is shown at the top of every rate page.

What we are not

We do not buy or sell gold or silver. We only share rates and simple guides. The price at your shop is the final price.
