We show one benchmark rate for the whole country. Counter prices in Suha Bazar and Rang Mahal can be a little different from shop to shop, mostly in the buying price. Ask two shops before you decide.

Selling price is what a dealer asks when you buy. Buying price is what a dealer pays when you sell. Prices are for metal only and do not include making charges, tax or packing.

Lahore Sarafa markets

Suha Bazar and the lanes around Rang Mahal are the old centre of Lahore's gold and silver trade. Many wholesale dealers sit here, and small shops buy their stock from them. Prices are close to the national benchmark, with small differences by shop.

Tips for buyers in Lahore

  • Ask for the rate per tola and the total for your weight, then check it yourself.
  • Compare a sealed bar and Rawa side by side.
  • Ask the shop what it pays to buy back, so you know your margin.
  • Keep your bill and the original pack of any sealed bar.
