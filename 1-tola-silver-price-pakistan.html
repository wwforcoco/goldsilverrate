Selling price is what a dealer asks when you buy. Buying price is what a dealer pays when you sell. Prices are for metal only and do not include making charges, tax or packing.

Rawa and bar: why two prices

Today the PSL bar rate is Rs 560 higher per tola than Rawa. That extra amount pays for the sealed pack, the marked weight and easier resale. If you only need silver metal, for example to make a small piece, Rawa is the cheaper way to buy.

Small weights

1 Tola = 12 Masha and 1 Masha = 8 Ratti. So 1 Masha of PSL silver is about Rs 475. For anything else use the calculator.

Good to know

  • Small pieces often cost more per tola because of packing and handling.
  • The dealer buying price is lower than the selling price. The gap today is Rs 150 per tola on PSL.
  • Rates change during the day, so check the update time above.
