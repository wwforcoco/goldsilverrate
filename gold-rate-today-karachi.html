Why Karachi sets the benchmark

Karachi is the trading heart of the Pakistani bullion market. The Sarafa Bazar around Bolton Market and Saddar concentrates the country's biggest wholesale counters, bullion dealers and refining workshops, so the morning announcement made here becomes the reference line that Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and the rest of the country quote against. When a rate board in another city says "Karachi rate", it means the number fixed here at opening.

The city also hosts the deepest retail market. Sealed branded bars, QR-coded silver biscuits and imported gold bars all pass through Karachi counters first, which keeps the buyback spreads here among the tightest in Pakistan. For a seller with sealed, certified metal, the Karachi counter is usually the fastest route to a clean deal.

Counter notes for the Karachi buyer

  • Check the morning rate before you travel: the announcement moves daily with the world price and the rupee.
  • Wholesale vs retail lines: Saddar counters quote wholesale for bulk; retail showrooms quote per-piece with making charges.
  • Verify seals on the spot: QR-coded bars are standard here; scan before you pay.
  • Compare two counters: even inside one bazaar, premiums move from shop to shop.
