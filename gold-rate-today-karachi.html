We show one benchmark rate for the whole country. Counter prices in Bolton Market and Saddar can be a little different from shop to shop, mostly in the buying price. Ask two shops before you decide.

Selling price is what a dealer asks when you buy. Buying price is what a dealer pays when you sell. The 24K buying and selling rates and the 22K and 21K selling rates come from the market sheet. The other buying prices are worked out from the 24K gap, and 18K is 75% of 24K. Always confirm the final price at the counter.

Where Karachi buys and sells gold

Karachi has two well-known gold areas. Bolton Market is the old wholesale centre, where dealers trade in bulk. Saddar has many retail jewellers, so most families buy there. Retail shops add their own margin on top of the wholesale price.

Tips for buyers in Karachi

  • Visit at least two shops and ask the same question: rate for your karat and weight.
  • Ask what the shop pays if you sell back the same day.
  • Get a written bill with the shop name and address.
  • For big purchases, prefer a shop that has been in the market for years.
