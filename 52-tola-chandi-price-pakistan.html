Why 52 and 52.5 tola?

Bulk silver is often quoted in lots of about 52 tola. Some pieces come slightly heavier, so you will also see 52.5 tola. Always weigh the piece on a scale and pay for the actual weight.

Buying bulk silver safely

  • Fix the weight and the rate in writing before payment.
  • Ask for a purity test in front of you for Rawa.
  • Check if the price is per tola or for the whole lot.
  • Do not pay the full amount in cash without a signed bill.

Fine silver or Rawa?

999 fine silver is purer and easier to sell later. Rawa costs less per tola but its purity depends on the refiner. For large amounts, a small purity gap becomes a big money gap, so test it.
