What the karat number means

Karat tells you how much of the piece is pure gold. 24K is pure. 22K is 22 parts gold out of 24, so about 91.6%. 21K is 87.5% and 18K is 75%. The rest is another metal such as copper that makes the gold harder.

Which karat is used for what

  • 24K: gold bars and coins, because it is pure.
  • 22K: most jewellery sets in Pakistan.
  • 21K: everyday jewellery that needs to be a bit stronger.
  • 18K: light and designer pieces, often with stones.

Buying gold: simple rules

  1. Fix the karat and the weight before you talk about price.
  2. Ask for the rate of the day and compare it with this page.
  3. For jewellery, ask the kaat (wastage) and mazdoori (making) separately.
  4. Take a bill that shows the karat, weight, rate and making charge.
  5. Keep the bill. When you sell, the shop will ask for it.
