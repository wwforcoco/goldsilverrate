How we work out the kilo price

1 kg is 1000 grams. One tola is 11.664 grams, so 1 kg equals 1000 divided by 11.664, which is about 85.73 tola. We multiply that by the tola rate.

Where 1 kg bars are used

  • Savings: a single large bar is easier to store than many small pieces.
  • Factories: silver is used in electronics, solar panels and parts that need to conduct electricity well.
  • Jewellery makers: large casting orders are bought by the kilo.

What to check on a kilo bar

Look for the purity stamp (999), the weight mark and a serial number. Ask for an assay or purity slip if the bar is not from a sealed brand. For big money, test the bar at a second shop before you pay.
