What a PSL bar actually is

PSL bars are factory-cast silver bars in fixed weights, most popularly 10 tola (116.64 grams). Each bar is cast from 999 fine silver, sealed at the factory, and packed with a certificate card that repeats the weight, purity and a serial number. A QR code on the packing lets you confirm that serial against the record. Because the seal proves the bar has not been touched since casting, dealers across the Sarafa market accept it quickly, and the buyback gap stays small.

The 10 tola size sits in the sweet spot for Pakistani buyers: big enough to matter as a store of value, small enough to sell one bar when you need cash without touching the rest of your holding.

How to check authenticity before you pay

  1. Seal: the factory seal must be unbroken and untampered. Any re-sealed packing is a warning sign.
  2. Card match: the weight, purity and serial on the certificate card must match what is stamped on the bar itself.
  3. QR scan: scan the QR code on the spot. It should return the same serial and weight you are holding.
  4. Weigh it: ask for the bar on the counter scale. Ten tola must show about 116.64 grams, within tolerance.
  5. Bill: take a proper bill with the bar serial written. Your future buyback starts from that receipt.

If any of these checks fails, walk away. The Sarafa market always has another sealed bar; a doubtful one is never worth the discount.
