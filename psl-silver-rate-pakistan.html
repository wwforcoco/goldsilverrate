How to check a PSL bar before you pay

  1. Look at the seal. The pack should be closed, with no cuts, glue marks or loose corners.
  2. Scan the QR code. Compare the details you see with the printed weight and serial number on the pack and on the bar.
  3. Match the serial number. The number on the bar, the pack and the bill should be the same.
  4. Weigh it. Ask the dealer to put it on a digital scale in front of you. A 10 tola bar should read close to 116.64 grams.
  5. Take a proper bill. Write the date, weight and rate on it. Keep it with the original pack.

Why people choose sealed bars

With loose silver you must trust the shop to test it every time. A sealed bar already has the weight and purity written on it, so the next buyer can check it quickly. That makes it easier to sell later, and usually at a clear rate.

Selling your bar back

Keep the pack and the bill. A bar in its original seal usually gets a better buying price than a bar with a broken pack. Ask two or three dealers for their buying rate before you decide.
