What each brand offers

Pakistani counters stock sealed bullion from several makers, and the differences sit in casting, certification and resale recognition, not in the metal itself. All quoted bars are 999 fine silver or 999.9 fine gold unless a page says otherwise.

How to judge any sealed bar

Whatever the brand, the checks are the same five. One: the factory seal is unbroken. Two: the certificate card matches the bar's stamped weight, purity and serial. Three: any QR or veri-scanner code returns the same serial you hold. Four: the bar weighs true on a calibrated scale. Five: the bill names the brand, serial, weight and rate. A bar that passes all five sells back at the tightest spreads in the market; a bar that fails any one of them is a discount trap, not a bargain.

On resale, recognition matters as much as purity. Locally recognised brands like PSL and KBE move fastest inside the Sarafa market, while imported names like PAMP carry global recognition for buyers who may deal abroad. Both routes are valid; choose by where you expect to sell.
