Sealed bar brands you will see

ARY, PSL and KBE are well-known names in Pakistan's bullion market. PAMP is a Swiss refiner whose bars are sold in a sealed assay card. All four sell bars that come in a sealed pack with the weight and purity marked on it.

Buying tips

  • Buy from a shop that has a fixed place and a name you can trace.
  • Do not buy a bar with a pack that looks opened or re-sealed.
  • Ask what the shop pays to buy the same bar back.
  • Keep the pack, bill and bar together in a safe place.

Common questions

What is the price of a gold bar in Pakistan today?

Pure 24K gold benchmark is Rs 421,000 per tola. A brand bar may add a small premium.

How do I check a KBE or PSL silver bar?

Check the seal, scan the code if there is one, match the serial number and weigh it before paying.

Which bar is easiest to resell?

Bars in their original sealed pack with the bill are easiest to resell.
