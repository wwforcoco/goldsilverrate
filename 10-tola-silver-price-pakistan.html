Selling price is what a dealer asks when you buy. Buying price is what a dealer pays when you sell. Prices are for metal only and do not include making charges, tax or packing.

Bar or biscuit: which one to buy

A silver biscuit is a flat cast piece sold in the Sarafa Bazar. A sealed bar from a brand such as PSL or KBE comes in a pack. Both are 10 tola if marked so, but the price is different.

Bar versus local biscuit
Weight / UnitSelling Price (PKR)Buying Price (PKR)
Gap on 10 tola (Bar minus Rawa)Rs 5,700Rs 5,500

The gap is what you pay for the sealed pack and easier resale. For long saving, many people like the sealed bar. For gifting or jewellery work, local biscuits are fine.

Buyback margin

The difference between the selling and buying price is the dealer margin. Today on a PSL 10 tola bar it is Rs 1,500. Keep the margin in mind: silver should rise by at least that much before you break even.

Tips before you buy

  • Ask for the weight on a digital scale.
  • Get a bill with date, weight and rate.
  • Keep the original pack for the PSL bar.
  • Compare two shops before you pay.
