How the Karachi silver counter works

Karachi's silver trade runs on two parallel lines. The wholesale counters around the main Sarafa bazaar move kilogram bars and bulk lots on the 999 fine benchmark, supplying workshops across Sindh and beyond. The retail counters in Saddar and neighbourhood bazaars sell single tola bars, biscuits and silver jewellery on the Rawa line, adding counter premiums and work charges for small pieces.

Because Karachi is the entry point for sealed branded stock, its counters usually hold the freshest supply of QR-coded bars. For buyers that means two things: the widest choice of verifiable bars, and the tightest buyback in the country for certified metal. The same morning benchmark fixes both lines; the difference at the counter comes from premiums, not from the metal itself.

Practical notes

  • Name the line: say clearly whether you want 999 fine bar metal or Rawa jewellery metal; the quotes differ.
  • Bulk gets a talk: premiums on multi-bar purchases are negotiable at Karachi wholesale counters.
  • Scan every QR: factory seals and serials are your resale guarantee; check them under counter light before paying.
