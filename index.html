What makes these two rates

Every metal in the Pakistani Sarafa market carries two numbers, not one. The selling rate is the counter price when you buy bars or jewellery. The buying rate (buyback) is what a dealer offers when you sell. The gap between them covers dealer handling, testing and counter costs. For sealed branded bars like PSL 10 tola, the gap stays tight because the bar is easy to verify with its QR code and seal.

Local refined silver, called Rawa, trades at a step below sealed 999 fine bars. Rawa is what most city bazaars use for everyday jewellery and utensils. Both rates come from the morning Sarafa Bazar benchmark and move together with the world price and the rupee-dollar exchange rate.
