Why Sarafa Bazar rates differ from news rates

News sites often show only the 24K gold rate, or the world price changed into rupees. When you walk into a Sarafa Bazar shop, you see something else: a rate for the bar, a rate for loose Rawa, a selling price and a lower buying price. This site shows all of them together, so you know what to expect at the counter.

How to use these rates

  1. Find your weight in the tables above.
  2. Check the update time at the top.
  3. Visit two shops and ask for the same karat or bar.
  4. Take a bill with date, weight and rate.

The difference between selling and buying price is the dealer margin. It is the amount the price has to rise before you break even, so keep it in mind.
