Three kinds of silver you will hear about

Pure 999 fine silver

This is silver that is 99.9% pure. It is sold as sealed bars and bullion pieces, and it is the form most people use to save money in silver. Dealers pay a little less when you sell it back, which is why you see two prices.

Local Rawa silver

Rawa is the silver sold loose in the Sarafa Bazar by weight. It is cheaper per tola than a sealed bar because there is no bar premium, but you need a dealer you trust to test the purity.

PSL sealed bars

These come in sealed packs with a QR code. Because the weight and purity are already marked, they are easy to resell. See our PSL silver bar page for the buying checklist.

What moves the silver price in Pakistan

  • The international silver price, which changes through the day.
  • The dollar to rupee rate. When the dollar goes up, silver costs more rupees.
  • Demand from factories, since silver is used in electronics and solar panels.
  • Local demand and supply in the big Sarafa markets.

Because of these four things the rate can change more than once in a day. We show one benchmark rate with the time it was updated, so you always know how fresh it is. We do not keep a past rate archive on this page yet, so check the update time before you decide.

Common questions

What is the silver rate in Pakistan today?

The PSL bar rate today is Rs 5,850 per tola selling and Rs 5,700 per tola buying. Local Rawa is Rs 5,280 selling and Rs 5,150 buying. Last updated: Today 13 Nov 2026, 11:30 AM PKT.

Why is Rawa cheaper than a PSL bar?

A PSL bar carries a premium for the sealed pack, the QR code and the easy resale. Rawa is sold loose by weight, so the price per tola is lower.

How many grams are in one tola of silver?

One tola is 11.664 grams. Ten tola is 116.64 grams.
