What are kaat and mazdoori?

Kaat is the wastage the jeweller keeps for metal lost while making the piece. It is usually counted in masha or ratti per tola. Mazdoori is the making charge, usually a fixed amount per tola or a percentage. Both are on top of the metal price, so ask your jeweller to tell you each number clearly.

Example

A 2 tola 22K gold set with 0.5 masha kaat per tola and Rs 5,000 mazdoori per tola costs the metal value, plus the kaat in gold, plus Rs 10,000 making. Put your own numbers into the estimator to see the total.
