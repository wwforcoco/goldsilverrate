How the calculator computes

The engine converts your weight into grams first (one tola is 11.664 grams, one masha is a twelfth of a tola, one ratti is an eighth of a masha), then multiplies by the per gram counter line of the metal you picked. All lines come from the same data/rates.json file that powers every rate table on this website, so the calculator never drifts from the numbers on the boards. The result is the metal value at the board rate; add your shop's making charges separately, or use the kaat estimator above.
